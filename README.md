Rune Clash — an original turn-based card duel. Lyra, the Ember Scribe, faces the Warden of the Hollow across a felt table: summon creatures of Ember, Tide, Leaf and Stone, cast spells, and bring the Warden's 20 life to zero before yours falls. Keyboard and mouse, no network, no account — just the table.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a card, then its target; keys 1–8 pick a hand card, Space ends your turn, Enter starts or restarts, M toggles sound.
